Architecture¶

Architecture elements (arch) of the Charging Controller (CHG), each satisfying vehicle requirements. Workflow stage archs, stream chg. The whole folder exists only in battery-electric products.

arch High-power charging interface ARCH_CHG_INLET ¶
Details
statusdraft
satisfiesREQ_MAX_CHARGE_POWER, REQ_MCS_CHARGING
affected_byDEC_CHARGING_INLET_PRIORITY
gapsGAP_ARCH_CHG_INLET_REVIEW__TRUCK_BEV_NMC_EU
refined_bySWREQ_CHG_INTERFACE_DERATING, SWREQ_CHG_MCS_HANDSHAKE

The high-power charging interface is an MCS vehicle inlet (IEC 63379) next to the CCS inlet, rated for the charging power of REQ_MAX_CHARGE_POWER, with connector lock and pin temperature monitoring.

arch CCS inlet and charging session ARCH_CHG_SESSION_CONTROL ¶
Details
statusdraft
satisfiesREQ_EU_CHARGING_STANDARD
gapsGAP_ARCH_CHG_SESSION_CONTROL_REVIEW__TRUCK_BEV_NMC_EU
refined_bySWREQ_CHG_CURRENT_LIMIT, SWREQ_CHG_SESSION_START

The CCS inlet is a CCS2 vehicle inlet (IEC 62196-3). The charging session control runs the session with the charger over ISO 15118-2 and sets the charging current limit from the BMS cell limits.
