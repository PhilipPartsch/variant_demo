System overview¶

The system architecture of the CV platform: the vehicle as system (system) and its subsystems (subsystem). The vehicle level is the system level: the user stories and vehicle requirements are the stakeholder and system requirements of the vehicle. Each subsystem covers the vehicle requirements allocated to it; its architecture elements are part_of it. Workflow stage subsystems.

Details
statusdraft
has_partSUB_BMS, SUB_CHG, SUB_VCU

The commercial vehicle of this product (truck_bev_nmc_eu): vehicle truck, powertrain bev, market eu. Which subsystems it contains depends on its powertrain.

subsystem Vehicle Control Unit (VCU) SUB_VCU ¶
Details
statusdraft
coversREQ_ENERGY_SOURCE, REQ_HV_SHUTDOWN_ON_ISOLATION_FAULT, REQ_RANGE_ESTIMATE, REQ_TRACTION_POWER_LIMIT
part_ofSYS_VEHICLE
gapsGAP_SUB_VCU_REVIEW__TRUCK_BEV_NMC_EU
has_partARCH_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY, ARCH_VCU_HV_SHUTDOWN, ARCH_VCU_POWER_MGMT

The VCU shows the remaining energy and range and limits the traction power in every product, coordinates the high-voltage shutdown in battery-electric products and the door interlock in buses. Own documentation and C code (src/vcu).

subsystem Charging Controller (CHG) SUB_CHG ¶
Details
statusdraft
coversREQ_EU_CHARGING_STANDARD, REQ_MAX_CHARGE_POWER, REQ_MCS_CHARGING
part_ofSYS_VEHICLE
gapsGAP_SUB_CHG_REVIEW__TRUCK_BEV_NMC_EU
has_partARCH_CHG_INLET, ARCH_CHG_SESSION_CONTROL

The CHG charges the battery at the rated charging power through the CCS inlet of the market (ISO 15118 session) and the high-power interface of the product (MCS inlet, roof pantograph or none). Battery-electric products only; own documentation and C code (src/chg).

subsystem Battery Management System (BMS) SUB_BMS ¶
Details
statusdraft
coversREQ_ENERGY_SOURCE, REQ_HV_SHUTDOWN_ON_ISOLATION_FAULT, REQ_MAX_CHARGE_POWER, REQ_TRACTION_POWER_LIMIT
part_ofSYS_VEHICLE
has_partBB_CHARGE_LIMITS, BB_ENERGY_STATE, BB_HV_PROTECTION, BB_POWER_LIMITS

The BMS provides the state of charge, the permitted discharge power, the isolation fault with the main contactors and the cell limits for charging. Battery-electric products only; a separate product whose released needs are imported for the cell chemistry of the product (BMS release 0.1.0).
