Vehicle requirements¶

Vehicle and system requirements (req), each tracing to a user story. Product-specific values are held in the value field. Workflow stage reqs.

Energy and range¶

req Remaining energy display REQ_ENERGY_SOURCE ¶
Details
statusdraft
traces_toUS_RANGE_AWARENESS
gapsGAP_REQ_ENERGY_SOURCE_REVIEW__TRUCK_BEV_NMC_EU
satisfied_byARCH_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY, BB_ENERGY_STATE

The vehicle shall display the remaining energy as the state of charge reported by the battery management system, in percent with a resolution of 1 %.

req Range estimate REQ_RANGE_ESTIMATE ¶
Details
statusdraft
traces_toUS_RANGE_AWARENESS
satisfied_byARCH_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY

The vehicle shall display the estimated remaining range in kilometres with a resolution of 1 km, calculated from the remaining energy and the average consumption of the last 50 km (the nominal consumption until 50 km are driven). Energy source of this product: bev.

Traction power¶

req Traction power limit REQ_TRACTION_POWER_LIMIT ¶
Details
statusdraft
value450 kW
traces_toUS_PREDICTABLE_POWER
satisfied_byARCH_VCU_POWER_MGMT, BB_POWER_LIMITS

The vehicle shall limit the traction power measured at the drive axle to the value of this requirement, with a tolerance of 5 %.

Charging and high voltage¶

req Maximum charging power REQ_MAX_CHARGE_POWER ¶
Details
statusdraft
value1000 kW
traces_toUS_FAST_DEPOT_CHARGING
satisfied_byARCH_CHG_INLET, BB_CHARGE_LIMITS

The vehicle shall accept an average charging power of at least the value of this requirement while charging from 20 % to 80 % state of charge at a battery temperature of 25 °C.

req High-voltage shutdown on isolation fault REQ_HV_SHUTDOWN_ON_ISOLATION_FAULT ¶
Details
statusdraft
traces_toUS_HV_SAFETY
mitigated_byRISK_HV_EXPOSURE
satisfied_byARCH_VCU_HV_SHUTDOWN, BB_HV_PROTECTION

The vehicle shall open both poles of the high-voltage battery connection within 100 ms after the battery management system sets the isolation fault.

req Megawatt charging REQ_MCS_CHARGING ¶
Details
statusdraft
traces_toUS_FAST_DEPOT_CHARGING
satisfied_byARCH_CHG_INLET

The vehicle shall support charging sessions with the Megawatt Charging System (MCS): vehicle inlet according to IEC 63379 and charging communication according to ISO 15118-20.
