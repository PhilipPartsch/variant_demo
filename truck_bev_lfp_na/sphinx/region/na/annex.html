Market annex: North America¶

Regulations and charging standards for the North American market (MARKET__REGION__NA). The file exists only in NA products; the charging requirement inside it only in battery-electric ones (combined gating).

Requirement: North American charging standard REQ_NA_CHARGING_STANDARD
status: draft
traces_to: US_MARKET_CHARGING
satisfied_by: ARCH_CHG_SESSION_CONTROL

The vehicle shall provide a CCS1 (Combined Charging System, type 1) charging inlet according to SAE J1772.
