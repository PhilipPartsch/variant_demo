Software requirements¶

Software requirements (swreq) of the Vehicle Control Unit (VCU), each refining an architecture element. Workflow stage swreqs, stream vcu.

swreq Send remaining energy SWREQ_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY ¶
Details
statusdraft
refinesARCH_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY
implements_backIMPL_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY
verifies_backTEST_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY

The VCU software shall send the remaining energy of the energy source to the instrument cluster in percent, rounded to 1 %, once per second.

swreq Apply traction power limit SWREQ_VCU_POWER_LIMIT_APPLY ¶
Details
statusdraft
refinesARCH_VCU_POWER_MGMT
implements_backIMPL_VCU_POWER_LIMIT_APPLY
verifies_backTEST_VCU_POWER_LIMIT_APPLY

The VCU software shall limit the torque request to the active power limit divided by the current motor speed.

swreq Request contactor opening on isolation fault SWREQ_VCU_HV_SHUTDOWN ¶
Details
statusdraft
refinesARCH_VCU_HV_SHUTDOWN
implements_backIMPL_VCU_HV_SHUTDOWN
verifies_backTEST_VCU_HV_SHUTDOWN

The VCU software shall send the contactor-open request to the BMS within 10 ms after receiving the isolation fault.
