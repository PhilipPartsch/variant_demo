Vehicle requirements¶

Vehicle and system requirements (req), each tracing to a user story. Product-specific values are held in the value field. Workflow stage reqs.

Energy and range¶

Requirement: Remaining energy display REQ_ENERGY_SOURCE
status: draft
traces_to: US_RANGE_AWARENESS
satisfied_by: ARCH_ENG_FUEL_LEVEL, ARCH_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY

The vehicle shall display the remaining energy as the fuel tank level, in percent of the tank capacity with a resolution of 1 %.
Requirement: Range estimate REQ_RANGE_ESTIMATE
status: draft
traces_to: US_RANGE_AWARENESS
satisfied_by: ARCH_VCU_ENERGY_DISPLAY

The vehicle shall display the estimated remaining range in kilometres with a resolution of 1 km, calculated from the remaining energy and the average consumption of the last 50 km (the nominal consumption until 50 km are driven). Energy source of this product: diesel.

Traction power¶

Requirement: Traction power limit REQ_TRACTION_POWER_LIMIT
status: draft
value: 450 kW
traces_to: US_PREDICTABLE_POWER
satisfied_by: ARCH_ENG_TORQUE_INTERFACE, ARCH_VCU_POWER_MGMT

The vehicle shall limit the traction power measured at the drive axle to the value of this requirement, with a tolerance of 5 %.
